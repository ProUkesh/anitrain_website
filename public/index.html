App Features

Explore the features that make training fun and effective.

  • Anime Character Workouts

    Train with Saitama, Goku, Luffy, and more with routines inspired by their unique training styles.

  • XP & Level-Up System

    Earn XP, level up, and unlock new modes by consistently completing workouts.

  • Weekly Challenge: Yoga & Running

    Choose from 100+ 7-day plans including focused yoga, running, HIIT, and recovery modes.

  • Streak & Beast Mode

    Stay motivated with streak rewards and intense anime-themed beast mode challenges.

  • Train Multiple Characters

    Add up to 5 anime characters to your active roster and switch workouts anytime.

  • Smart Workout AI (Beta)

    Get AI-generated plans based on your level, goals, and anime preferences — like having a virtual coach.
