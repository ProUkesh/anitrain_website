FAQ

Quick answers about AniTrain, anime workouts, XP, streaks, and weekly challenges.

What is AniTrain?

AniTrain is an anime fitness app that gamifies training with XP, levels, streak boosts, and weekly challenges inspired by popular anime heroes.

Is AniTrain good for beginners?

Yes. You can start with beginner-friendly sessions and progress through harder workouts as your XP grows.

Does AniTrain include weekly challenges?

Yes. Weekly challenges can include HIIT, yoga, running, and other training styles to keep you consistent.

How do XP and levels work?

Complete workouts and challenges to earn XP. XP raises your level and unlocks rewards that keep motivation high.
