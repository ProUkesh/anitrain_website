About AniTrain

AniTrain is an anime-inspired fitness app designed to turn training into a game — earn XP, build streaks, and become a true warrior.

Why AniTrain exists

Most fitness apps feel repetitive. AniTrain was built for people who love anime, progress systems, and motivation. Instead of “just a workout”, you get a journey: pick a warrior, train daily, gain XP, and level up.

The goal is simple: make consistency fun — and make improvement feel rewarding.

About the developer

AniTrain is developed by an independent app developer focused on building polished, user-first apps. The project is built through consistent iteration, real testing, and long-term feature expansion.

AniTrain is not just a fitness app — it’s a motivation system designed to push users to become stronger in real life.
