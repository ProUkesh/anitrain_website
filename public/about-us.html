About AniTrain

AniTrain is an anime-inspired fitness app that turns training into a game — earn XP, build streaks, and level up your real life.

Why AniTrain exists

Most fitness apps feel repetitive. AniTrain was built for people who love anime, progress systems, and motivation. Instead of “just a workout,” you get a journey: pick a warrior, train daily, gain XP, and keep your streak alive.
