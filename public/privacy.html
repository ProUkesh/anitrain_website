Privacy Policy

This page explains how AniTrain collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective date: Last updated:

Quick Summary

  • • We collect only what we need to run the app, improve features, and keep things secure.
  • • We do not sell your personal information.
  • • Purchases are handled by the app store (Google Play / Apple App Store) and we receive limited transaction info.
  • • You can request deletion of your account data (see “Contact Us”).

1) Who We Are

AniTrain is an anime-inspired fitness application and website (“AniTrain”, “we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy applies to our website and mobile app, and related services.

2) Information We Collect

A. Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect information such as your email address, nickname/username, and profile/avatar settings.

B. Fitness & App Usage Data

We may collect app usage and engagement data such as workouts completed, XP/level progress, streaks, challenge progress, feature interactions, and in-app preferences.

Note: This app is fitness-focused. It is not intended for medical diagnosis or emergency services.

C. Device & Technical Data

We may collect device information like device model, OS version, language, app version, crash logs, and performance data to keep the app stable and secure.

D. Purchases & Subscriptions

When you purchase a subscription or unlock content, payment is processed by Google Play and/or Apple. We may receive purchase status (e.g., active subscription, product ID, purchase time) to unlock features. We do not receive your full card/banking information.

E. Community Content (If You Post)

If the app includes community posts/comments, we store what you choose to publish (for example: post text, comments, likes). Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public areas.

3) How We Use Your Information

  • • Provide and operate AniTrain features (workouts, streaks, XP, challenges, community).
  • • Personalize your experience (saved preferences, selected characters, progress).
  • • Process and verify purchases and subscriptions to unlock content.
  • • Improve performance, troubleshoot bugs, and enhance security.
  • • Communicate with you about updates, support, and important notices.

4) Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in these cases:

  • • Service providers that help us operate the app (e.g., hosting, analytics, crash reporting, databases).
  • • App stores (Google Play / Apple) to process purchases and subscriptions.
  • • Legal and safety if required by law or to protect users and prevent fraud/abuse.

5) Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion of your account data (see “Contact Us”).

6) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7) Health-Related Disclosures

AniTrain may include fitness tracking and wellness features. This information is used to provide app features such as progress tracking, XP, and streaks.

AniTrain is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. If you have health concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

8) Children’s Privacy

AniTrain is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9) Your Rights & Choices

  • • Access, correct, or delete your account information (where available).
  • • Request deletion of stored profile or progress data (subject to legal/operational limits).
  • • Manage subscription in your App Store / Google Play account settings.

10) International Users

If you use AniTrain from outside the United States, your information may be processed in countries where data protection laws may differ. By using AniTrain, you consent to these transfers.

11) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of AniTrain after changes means you accept the updated policy.
