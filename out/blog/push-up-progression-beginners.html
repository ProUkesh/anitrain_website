Push-up progression for beginners

The best push-up variation is the one you can repeat with control today and progress tomorrow.

Start at the right angle

Wall and elevated push-ups reduce the amount of bodyweight you need to move. Pick a height that lets you keep a straight body position and complete clean repetitions without rushing.

Make the same version stronger

Before moving to a harder variation, add a few good repetitions, use a slower lowering phase or pause briefly near the bottom. Better control is a real form of progression.

Lower the surface gradually

When an elevated push-up becomes comfortable, use a slightly lower sturdy surface. Small changes are easier to recover from than jumping from a wall directly to difficult floor sets.

Treat floor push-ups as another level

Once you can perform a few controlled floor repetitions, keep them clean and build volume over time. You can still use an easier variation afterward for additional practice.

“Earn the floor variation by owning an easier version first.”
